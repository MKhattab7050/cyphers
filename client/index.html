Why Cyphers?

We offer various features for chatting safely and exchanging data with P2P without feeling that your data is seen.

End-to-End Encryption

Ensures only the sender and receiver can read the messages & prevents interception by third parties during transmission.

AES-256

Cross platform

Can be used on desktop as an app & on the web on phones and tablets.

DesktopWebMobile

Video calls

Allows users to engage in real-time, face-to-face communication without relying on centralized servers.

Peer Discovery and NAT

Uses technologies like STUN (Session Traversal Utilities for NAT) and TURN (Traversal Using Relays around NAT).

STUNTURN

Screen Sharing

Adds functionality for collaboration, especially in professional or educational contexts.

File Transfer

Files are sent directly between peers without size restrictions imposed by servers.
